5 · every module on the same axis, one lane per module type

A weapon is one kind of module. The same rule prices every ShipModule in this content root: the cheapest total of tech costs from a free node to a tech whose ModulesUnlocked names it. 154 of 199 files are on this race's axis; the other 45 are listed by no tech, or only by secret ones, so they are acquired rather than researched.

A weapon module and its gun share a cost by construction; what this view adds is everything around them — armour, shields, reactors, fuel cells, engines, hangars, troops, colony and storage. The type is the file's ModuleType, not the weapon tag family above: a Turret is a mount, and the same mount holds kinetic, energy and beam guns.

7 · the ships this race actually ships with, on a bill of their own

A design's bill is the sum of the tech nodes in its build gate: ShipDesign.TechsNeeded is the hull's whole tree plus, for every unique module, the whole tree of the tech that unlocks it. Each tech is paid for once, so this is a sum and not the reach of any one node — which is why ships start far to the right of weapons: a hull and its support modules are most of the bill.
