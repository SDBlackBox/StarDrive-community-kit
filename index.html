A band is research points, not turns. “0–1,830” is everything reachable for 1,830 points after the free opening. What that costs in turns depends on the empire's net research per turn — colonies, research stations, alliances — which is an income question, not a tree question.

What the map already says

  • Beam is the largest type (20 of 95) and the strongest at the top. Disintegrator is 1,680 dps at 9,260 with 11,000 range. Beam pays in power rather than points: it draws 2,556/s the whole time it fires, so a beam fleet is a reactor budget.
  • Above 5,000 an era is one research line wide. Band 3 is Polaron Cannons alone and band 4 is BigCannon alone; eight of the ten weapon chains advance in neither.
  • Point defence finishes at 2,620 and is defence, not a damage lane. All six Human PD weapons are TruePD: they engage missiles and torpedoes and cannot fire at a ship at all.
  • Hull gates are cheaper than the plan first assumed. A 3×8 waits for the 1,600 station or the 1,680 LightCarrier, and a 4×4 turret fits the 480 Gunboat. Late-game themes are limited by cells and power, not by having bought the big hull.
  • A gap band is where defence and support tech go. Shields, armour, point defence, repair, inhibitor, carrier, power, engines — four kinds of line on four budgets, not one.

The mod and the race are arguments, not assumptions: a mod unifies content by file name and wins, and a race's secret, free and hidden techs change the ladder before any cost is quoted. Nothing on these pages is fetched at runtime, so a page that is out of date is out of date in the file.

The kit — 20 prompts

Each entry in the sidebar is a prompt you paste into a chat that has this repository in front of it. They are not wiki pages: they ask what you already know, read named files, answer from symbols rather than memory, and finish by reporting what they checked and what they could not.

This page and the prompt pages beside it are the kit's own repository. Clone it into a checkout with git clone https://github.com/SDBlackBox/StarDrive-community-kit.git docs/kit and each prompt sits where its citations expect it. Every prompt page has a Copy prompt button that puts the whole file on your clipboard — paste it, then ask your question. The text is embedded when the page is built, so the button needs no network and the page reads with JavaScript off.

Generated by build-site.py in this folder from the table in it and the prompt files beside it, which the pages embed at build time. Re-run python3 build-site.py after editing a prompt. Vetted against StarDrive@f575899fe on 2026-11-17; a page whose numbers disagree with the code is a bug in the page.
